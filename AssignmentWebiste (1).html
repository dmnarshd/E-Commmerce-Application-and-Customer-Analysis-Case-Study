    About Us

    Tech-Zone Gadgets is a student-founded startup project dedicated to expanding access to quality technology across Saudi Arabia. We’re senior MIS students at Prince Mohammed Bin Fahd University (PMU) who believe that reliable, affordable gadgets help students, professionals, and creators do their best work.

    Our focus is simple: make modern tech easier to get, easier to use, and fairly priced. We curate essential accessories—power, audio, smart home, and creator gear—and prioritize products that deliver real value in day-to-day Saudi life.
